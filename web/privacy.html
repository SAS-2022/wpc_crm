WPC CRM Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Wood Panels Co, Saudi Arabia

WPC CRM is an internal app for Wood Panels Co staff: sales executives, key account managers, sales managers and the general manager. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Accounts are created by the company; the app is not open to the public.

What we collect

DataDetailsWhy
AccountName, work email, phone number, role, your manager, monthly visit target. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.To sign you in and show the right features for your role.
LocationWhile you are signed in on a phone as a sales executive, key account manager or driver, the app sends your position every 30 seconds (the company can change this interval) during the company's work hours (currently Saturday to Thursday, 08:00–17:00 Saudi time, set by the company) or the tracking hours the company set for you, also when the app is closed. Drivers, who keep a company car, share their location at all times, also outside work hours, unless the company set other hours for them. Outside work hours no location is sent, your last position is removed from the live map, and you show as off duty. To restart sharing on time, the app keeps a low-accuracy location check running outside work hours; those positions are not sent or stored. Each point includes latitude, longitude, accuracy, speed, time and whether the phone reported a mock (fake) location. During work hours the app also records whether you are online, whether GPS is on, which location permission is granted, whether notifications are allowed for the app and, on Android, whether battery optimization is on for the app. These changes are kept in a tracking log, and your key account manager (if you have one), the sales managers and the general manager are notified if GPS is turned off, the permission is reduced, notifications are turned off, battery optimization is turned on, or your phone stays offline for 30 minutes of work time (the company can change this time).Live team map, daily route history, checking that visits took place at the client, and making sure tracking works during work hours.

The phone then reports only when you arrive at or leave the site, with the time, also when the app is closed; your position is not tracked anywhere else for attendance. The "Check in" button takes one location reading to confirm you are at the site. The company sees your check-in and check-out times and hours for each day.Recording attendance at the work site (check-in, check-out, working hours and overtime).
RegistrationIf you create an account yourself: your name, phone number, position, company and email address. The account stays inactive until the sales manager or general manager approves it.Setting up your account.
VisitsClient visited, time, check-in position and accuracy, distance from the client, notes, comments, and whether a manager joined the visit.Visit records, statistics and targets.

Plans and notificationsDaily plan items, tasks to do at clients with your comments and the time you completed them, approvals, and in-app notifications.Planning and approving visits.
DeviceA push notification token for your phone, its platform (Android or iOS) and your app language. Your language choice is also saved on the phone.Sending notifications in your language.
Client business dataCompany names, contact people, phone numbers, email and street addresses, map locations, categories and account balances of the company's customers, mostly imported from the company's Odoo system.Managing customer visits.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for anything outside the company's sales work.
  • The app does not read your phone's contacts, messages or microphone.

Who can see your data

  • You: your own clients, visits, plans and statistics.
  • Your key account manager: the data of the sales executives they supervise, including live location and route history.
  • Sales manager and general manager: the data of all staff.
  • Service providers: Google, which runs the services the app is built on (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging, and Google Maps). Push notifications to iPhones are delivered through Apple. These providers process data only to run the service.

Where the data is stored

Data is stored on Google Cloud servers in the United States and is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

How long we keep it

Route history (the positions sent every 30 seconds) is deleted automatically after 90 days, and the tracking log (online, GPS, permission and battery changes) after 30 days. Other data is kept while you work with the company and for as long as the company needs it for its sales records. When your account is removed, you can no longer sign in and your profile is deleted; visit records remain part of the company's sales history unless you ask for them to be deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • You can stop location sharing at any time by signing out, or by turning off location access for the app in your phone's settings. Some features, such as GPS check-in, will then not work.
  • You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or ask questions about this policy, by writing to wissam.b@woodpanelsco.com. We handle requests in line with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

ما لا نقوم به

  • لا إعلانات، ولا أدوات تتبع إعلانية أو تحليلية.
  • لا نبيع بياناتك ولا نؤجّرها، ولا نستخدمها لأي غرض خارج أعمال المبيعات في الشركة.
  • لا يقرأ التطبيق جهات الاتصال أو الرسائل أو الميكروفون في هاتفك.

من يمكنه الاطلاع على بياناتك

  • أنت: عملاؤك وزياراتك وخططك وإحصائياتك.
  • مدير الحسابات الرئيسية الخاص بك: بيانات مندوبي المبيعات الذين يشرف عليهم، بما في ذلك الموقع المباشر وسجل المسار.
  • مدير المبيعات والمدير العام: بيانات جميع الموظفين.
  • مزودو الخدمة: شركة Google التي تشغّل الخدمات التي بُني عليها التطبيق (Firebase Authentication وCloud Firestore وRealtime Database وCloud Storage وCloud Functions وCloud Messaging، وخرائط Google). وتُرسل الإشعارات إلى أجهزة iPhone عبر Apple. يعالج هؤلاء المزودون البيانات لتشغيل الخدمة فقط.

أين تُخزّن البيانات

تُخزّن البيانات على خوادم Google Cloud في الولايات المتحدة، وهي مشفّرة أثناء النقل (HTTPS) وأثناء التخزين.

مدة الاحتفاظ بالبيانات

يُحذف سجل المسار (المواقع المرسلة كل 30 ثانية) تلقائيًا بعد 90 يومًا، ويُحذف سجل التتبع (تغيّرات الاتصال وGPS والإذن والبطارية) بعد 30 يومًا. ونحتفظ بالبيانات الأخرى طوال فترة عملك مع الشركة، وطالما احتاجت الشركة إليها لسجلات المبيعات. عند إزالة حسابك لا يمكنك تسجيل الدخول ويُحذف ملفك الشخصي، وتبقى سجلات الزيارات جزءًا من سجل مبيعات الشركة ما لم تطلب حذفها.

خياراتك وحقوقك

  • يمكنك إيقاف مشاركة الموقع في أي وقت بتسجيل الخروج، أو بإيقاف إذن الموقع للتطبيق من إعدادات الهاتف. عندها لن تعمل بعض الميزات مثل تسجيل الحضور بالموقع.
  • يمكنك إيقاف الإشعارات من إعدادات الهاتف.
  • يمكنك طلب الاطلاع على بياناتك الشخصية أو تصحيحها أو حذفها، أو الاستفسار عن هذه السياسة، بالكتابة إلى wissam.b@woodpanelsco.com. نتعامل مع الطلبات وفق نظام حماية البيانات الشخصية في المملكة العربية السعودية.
